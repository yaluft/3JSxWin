2. RELAY — second ESP32, radio only

Same seven radio wires, same colors, same GPIO numbers. No GPS, no buttons — this one stays on Wi-Fi to the cloud. Screw the 433 antenna on before power; two antennas, both vertical, not touching each other.

3. 33 AWG without solder — make pins with folds

  1. Strip 12 mm. Enamel magnet wire: scrape until shiny copper.
  2. Fold in half, in half again (4 strands), twist tight — that is Dupont-pin thickness.
  3. Into a sacrificed female contact (panel 2), or through a module hole folded like a staple (panel 3).
  4. Do not tin. Do not use 5 V. Tug-test every wire before power.

4. Power-on checklist

  1. USB into the PC, not a charger brick, for the first test.
  2. Multimeter: E07 pin 2 to GND is ~3.3 V, never ~5 V.
  3. Antennas screwed on before power, both vertical, not touching.
  4. Wiggle each Dupont. If a 33 AWG stub pulls out, fold more strands.
  5. Do not flash yet — this page is the gate. Firmware comes after the map matches your silk.

Do not bolt onto this DevKit

  • Anything 5 V logic without a shifter (many Arduino sensor kits).
  • Modules that want GPIO 12 high at boot (SD on 12 bricks boot).
  • A second SPI radio (nRF24, RC522, extra CC1101) on the sniffer.
  • Solder — everything above is Dupont or 33 AWG stubs, like the rest of this page.
